Environment Switcher

Choose the environment a page opens on: after Preview or Publish in DA, and from the toolbar icon.

One per line: NAME = branch, for example UAT = uat. Branches are lower case: a-z, 0-9 and -.

One per line: the original site, then its EDS site, for example www.example.com = repo--org. With or without www.. Add a path to drop it from the URL: www.example.com/content/brand = repo--org.
